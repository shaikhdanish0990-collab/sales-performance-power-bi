# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard built with Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales, profitability, products, categories, payment methods, and sales channels.

The dashboard provides a simple management view of key sales KPIs and allows users to explore performance using interactive filters.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales transaction data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

The dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- How much total sales and profit are generated?
- How is sales performance changing month by month?
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- Which categories contribute the most revenue?
- Which payment mode generates more sales?
- Which sales type/channel performs best?
- How does daily sales performance change over time?
- What are the key sales and profitability trends?

---

## 🏢 Business Problem

Businesses often have sales data stored in transaction-level files, making it difficult to quickly identify trends and performance patterns.

This dashboard converts the raw data into a visual reporting solution that helps management:

- Monitor sales KPIs
- Track profitability
- Compare product performance
- Analyze category contribution
- Understand payment preferences
- Compare sales channels
- Identify sales trends and opportunities

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures and calculated columns
- **Data Modeling** – Relationships between transaction and master data
- **Microsoft Excel** – Source data and validation
- **Power BI Visualizations** – KPI Cards, Donut Charts, Bar Charts, Area Charts and Treemap

---
## 📊 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

The dashboard provides high-level KPIs including:

- 💰 Total Sales
- 📦 Total Quantity
- 💵 Total Profit
- 📈 Profit Margin %
- 🧾 Sales summary

### 📅 Sales & Profit by Month

A monthly visual shows sales and profit performance across the selected period, helping identify seasonal trends and changes in performance.

### 💳 Sales by Payment

A donut chart compares sales generated through:

- Online
- Cash

### 🛍️ Sales Products

A ranked bar chart highlights the products generating the highest sales.

### 📈 Daily Sales

An area chart shows daily sales movement and helps identify high and low performing days.

### 🔄 Sales by Type

A donut chart compares sales across different sales types:

- Direct Sales
- Online
- Wholesaler

### 🏷️ Sales Category

A treemap visualizes category-wise sales contribution and makes it easy to identify the strongest categories.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes slicers for:

- Year
- Month
- Sales Type
- Payment Mode

These filters allow users to dynamically analyze different periods, channels, and payment methods.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The dashboard can be used to identify:

- Top-performing products by sales
- Highest-contributing product categories
- Monthly sales and profit trends
- Daily sales fluctuations
- Preferred payment methods
- Best-performing sales channels
- Relationship between sales and profitability

The insights can support decisions related to product focus, sales strategy, channel performance, and business planning.
